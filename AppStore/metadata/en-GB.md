## Description

You are not bad with plants. You just couldn't see the problem in time.

By the time leaves yellow, whatever caused it happened weeks ago. Grove closes that gap. Photograph a plant and Grove identifies it, reads its condition, and gives you a small number of things to actually do — each one with the reason behind it.

Then it does the part no reminder app does: it looks again. On a schedule that follows the plant's health, Grove asks for one fresh photo and compares it to the last, so you find out whether what you did worked. Better, stable, or worse, in plain words, with the evidence it used.

WHAT IS THIS PLANT?
Point your camera at anything green. Grove gives you the most likely species, how confident it is, what separates it from its lookalikes, and what it needs. Not sure? Grove says so rather than guessing, and holds back species-specific advice until you confirm.

HOW IS MY PLANT REALLY DOING?
Every plant you own gets a living record: identity, environment, health baseline, every photo, every action, and who did it. Health is shown as a band and a direction of travel — Thriving, Healthy, Keep an eye on it, Needs attention, Urgent — always next to the reasons behind it.

WHAT DO I DO NEXT?
A short, prioritised list. Never busywork, never a blind watering schedule. Grove asks you to feel the soil before it ever asks you to water it, because overwatering kills more houseplants than drought does.

WHO LOOKS AFTER THEM WHEN I'M AWAY?
Hand chosen plants to a friend for chosen dates. They see only what's due and how to check it. Access expires by itself when the dates end. Care logged by a friend's Grove account is shown under their name; care from a link is recorded from the link. When a link ends you get a receipt: how many plants, how many care tasks, and whether the care sheet was opened.

ASK ARI, WHO KNOWS YOUR PLANT
Ari, Grove's botanist, answers your questions with that plant's own record attached — its health, its last few check-ins, the soil you reported, the care that's due. Not general advice about monstera; advice about yours. Ari suggests what's worth asking from what Grove has actually seen, says when the answer is uncertain, and sends you to a vet rather than guessing if a pet has had a chew. Three questions a day are free; Premium is unlimited.

IN THE APP
• Plant identification with confidence, alternatives, and honest uncertainty
• Photo health assessment with the evidence and the limits shown
• Your grove, with rooms, collections, search, and health trends
• Guided check-ins with before-and-after comparison
• Prioritised care tasks you can snooze with a reason
• Plant sitting with chosen plants, chosen dates, and access that expires by itself
• Ari answers from your plant's own history
• A milestone Journey with lessons, badges, and Gardener levels earned from real care
• Siri and Shortcuts actions to scan a plant, open a plant, and start a check-in
• Private by default, with a full local data export and account deletion in Settings

Identification and health guidance are a best guess from a photograph, not a diagnosis. Grove cannot see roots, pests under the soil, or a pot that never drains. Never eat, brew, or apply a plant to skin on the strength of a Grove match, and if a plant keeps declining while you follow the plan, ask someone who can inspect it in person.
